How I like to work

Principles I build by

Craft over shortcuts

I'd rather ship a smaller thing done properly than a big thing held together with hope.

Stay curious

New tools and patterns get evaluated on merit, not hype — but I try them before I judge them.

Communicate early

Blockers and trade-offs surface as soon as I see them — no surprises the day before a deadline.

Consistency compounds

Small, steady improvements to a codebase beat heroics — most of my best work is unglamorous.
